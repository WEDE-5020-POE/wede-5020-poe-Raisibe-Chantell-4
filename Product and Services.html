At RONALDO MCDONALD House Charities South Africa, we offer a range of programs and services designed to support families with hospitalised children. Oour program reduce the emotional and financial strain of having a hospitalised child by providing accommodation, family-centered support, and mobile health clinics. We are committed to keeping families together when it matters most.

in hospitals spaces where families can find support and comfort while their children are receiving medical care.

  • Ronald McDonald House - accommodation near hospitals
  • Ronald McDonald Family Room - support spaces within hospitals
  • Ronald McDonald Care Mobile - mobile health clinics

Impact: Our programs have a significant impact on the lives of families, providing them with the support and resources they need during challenging times.
