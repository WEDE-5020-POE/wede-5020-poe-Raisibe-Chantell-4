About RMHC South Africa

OUR MISSION

since 1994, RMHC SA’s core mission has been to keep families near their hospitalised children...

1,200+ families supported (RMHC South Africa, 2025c)

OUR VISION

A world where every child has the chance to heal and every family has the support they need.

OUR VALUES

Compassion: We are dedicated to providing a supportive and caring environment for families in need.

Collaboration: We work together with our partners, volunteers, and communities to achieve our mission and make a positive impact on the lives of children and their families.

Integrity: We operate with honesty, transparency, and accountability in all that we do, ensuring that our resources are used effectively to support our mission.

Innovation: We continuously seek new and creative ways to improve our services and support for families, embracing change and adapting to the evolving needs of our communities.
